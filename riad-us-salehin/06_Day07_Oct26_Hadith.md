## Hadith of the day: -->
> عن أبي طريفٍ عدِيِّ بن حاتمٍ الطائيِّ رضي الله عنه، قَالَ: سمعتُ رسولَ الله صلى الله عليه وسلم يقول: ((مَنْ حَلَفَ عَلَى يَمِينٍ ثُمَّ رَأَى أتْقَى للهِ مِنْهَا فَليَأتِ التَّقْوَى)). رواه مسلم. 
يعني أن من حلفَ على فعل شيء أو تركه، فرأى غيره خيرًا من التمادي على اليمين واتقى الله، فَعَلَهُ وَكفَّر عن يمينه.

-----

## English: -->
### Hadith 72 —-> The Fourth Hadith

Abu Ṭarīf ʿAdī ibn Ḥātim al-Ṭāʾī (may Allah be pleased with him) reported:

> The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: **“Whoever takes an oath to do something, then sees that something else is more God-conscious than fulfilling that oath, let him do what is more God-conscious.”**
> **Narrated by Muslim.**

Meaning: If someone swears an oath to do something or to refrain from doing something, and then realizes that doing something else is better and more pleasing to Allah than continuing to adhere to the oath, then **he should do what is more God-conscious and offer the expiation (kaffārah) for his oath.**
