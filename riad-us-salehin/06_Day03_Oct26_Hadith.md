## Hadith of the day: -->
> عن أبي هريرةَ رضي الله عنه عن النَّبيّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: ((إنَّ الله تَعَالَى يَغَارُ، وَغَيرَةُ الله تَعَالَى، أنْ يَأتِيَ المَرْءُ مَا حَرَّمَ الله عَلَيهِ)). متفق عَلَيهِ. 
و ((الغَيْرةُ)): بفتحِ الغين، وَأَصْلُهَا الأَنَفَةُ. 
في هذا الحديث: مراقبة الله تعالى والخوف من غضبه وعقوبته إذا انتُهكت محارمه.

----

### English: -->
### Hadith 64 —-> The Fifth

Abu Hurairah (may Allah be pleased with him) reported that the Prophet ﷺ said:

> **“Indeed, Allah has protective jealousy, and Allah’s protective jealousy is that a person commits what Allah has forbidden him.”**

**Agreed upon** — narrated by Al-Bukhari and Muslim.

**“Al-Ghayrah” (protective jealousy):** with a fatḥah on the letter **ghayn (غَيْرَة)**. Its original meaning is **anafa** — a sense of honor, aversion, and unwillingness to tolerate what is improper.

**Lesson of the hadith:**
It teaches us to remain conscious of Allah, to fear His displeasure and punishment, and to be careful not to violate the things He has forbidden.
