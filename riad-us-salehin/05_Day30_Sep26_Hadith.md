## Hadith of the day:
> «59» السادس: عن أبي خالد حَكيمِ بنِ حزامٍ رضي الله عنه قَالَ: قَالَ رسولُ الله صلى الله عليه وسلم: ((البَيِّعَانِ بالخِيَار مَا لَمْ يَتَفَرَّقَا، فَإنْ صَدَقا وَبيَّنَا بُوركَ لَهُمَا في بيعِهمَا، وإنْ كَذَبَا وَكَتَمَا مُحِقَتْ بركَةُ بَيعِهِما)). مُتَّفَقٌ عَلَيهِ. 
في هذا الحديث: فضل الصدق والحث عليه، وذم الكذب والتحذير منه، وإنه سبب لذهاب البركة. 
وفيه: دليل على ثبوت خيار المجلس للبائع والمشتري.

---

### English:
### Hadith 59 — Truthfulness in Business

Abu Khalid Hakim ibn Hizam (may Allah be pleased with him) reported that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said:

> **“The buyer and the seller have the option [to cancel the transaction] as long as they have not separated. If they are truthful and make everything clear, their transaction will be blessed for them; but if they lie and conceal things, the blessing of their transaction will be erased.”**
> **Agreed upon (Al-Bukhari and Muslim).**

**Explanation:**
This hadith highlights the virtue of **truthfulness** and encourages Muslims to be honest in their dealings. It also condemns lying and concealing defects or important information, as these cause the **blessing (barakah) of a transaction to disappear**.

It also establishes the principle of **Khiyār al-Majlis (خيار المجلس)**: the buyer and seller have the right to proceed with or cancel the transaction as long as they have not yet separated after concluding the sale.
